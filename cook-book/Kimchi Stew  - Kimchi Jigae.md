# Ingredients
* Kimchi, chopped
* Protein (Chicken, Beef, Pork, Tuna)
* Green Onion, Chopped seperate whites and greens
* Gochujang
* Gochugaru
* Water or Broth
* Tofu
* Fish Sauce
* Soy Sauce
* Sesame Oil
* Served with Rice

# Instructions
1. Preheat Pot with some neutral oil.
2. Add chopped kimchi and saute
3. Add protein and mix
4. Add gochujang and gochugaru to taste and mix
5. Add white part of chopped green onion
6. add tofu and mix
7. add water or broth mix
8. season to taste with soy sauce, fish sauce, and sesame oil
9. serve with rice
