# Ingredients
* 1 lemon, zest and juice
* ½ cup finely grated Parmesan (real parm, grate it yourself)
* ¼ cup mayonnaise
* 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
* 2 oil-packed anchovies, drained and finely chopped
* 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

# Instructions
1. Zest and juice the lemon into a bowl.
2. Add the Parmesan, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, anchovies, and garlic.
3. Stir until smooth, then season with salt and plenty of freshly ground black pepper.
